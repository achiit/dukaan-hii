Ramesh PatelOwner, Shree Krishna General Store

A small shop run on a big promise: no one leaves unhappy.

My father, Krishnakant Patel, opened this shop in 2004 with two shelves and one weighing scale. I used to sit on a stool by the counter after school and learn every customer’s name, and what their family liked to eat.

Twenty years later, I still weigh the first kilo of the day myself. The faces have grown up, but the promise is the same as my father’s. Fair weight, fair price, and a smile when you walk in.

If something is not right, tell me. I will fix it before you reach home.

  • Honest weight, every time
  • Free home delivery within 2 km
  • Monthly khata for regular families
  • Stock checked daily for freshness
  • Easy returns if you are not happy
